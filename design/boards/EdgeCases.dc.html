When things don't go to plan

GUEST SEES · SERVER SEES
01 · ITEM ADDED MID-PAYMENT
Bill updated. Dessert ($9.00) was added. Your total hasn't changed.

New items join the unpaid list. Anyone mid-checkout sees the banner; their claimed items and amount stay the same.

02 · CLAIMED BUT NOT PAID
Your hold ran out. Burger and ½ Nachos went back on the bill. Pick items again

Claims expire after 5 minutes with no payment, so items are never stuck as “taken”.

03 · PAYMENT FAILED
Card declined. Nothing was charged. Your items are released; try again or use another method. Try again

The server is only notified on confirmed payments, never on attempts.

04 · SPLIT MODE LOCKED
Split 3 ways: your share is $32.01 1 of 3 paid · Pay for my items is off for this table

After the first payment, the table keeps one mode. Mixing equal and itemized shares can't be reconciled.

05 · PARTY LEFT WITH A BALANCE
Table 4 · $22.00 unpaid Pasta still open. No payment in 15 min.

Server-only alert. The server settles it at the POS before closing the table.

06 · ONE PERSON PAYS FOR ALL Pay whole bill$96.05

One tap ticks every unpaid item, then goes straight to the tip screen. Same itemized math, no extra logic.
